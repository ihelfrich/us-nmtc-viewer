**What changed since the first SSRN-ready draft (14--16 August 2026),
worth knowing before you post:**
- The paper now carries a positive finding as well as a null. Within
  intermediary, rural commercial real-estate rehabilitation shows a rural
  coefficient of -0.4393 (CDE-clustered SE 0.1019, p = 1.6e-05), the only
  cell of twenty in the subgroup scan to survive a Benjamini-Hochberg
  correction. It is reported as exploratory, because the purpose dimension
  was added after an earlier draft flagged real estate, and that provenance
  is stated in the abstract, Section 5.6 and the conclusion.
- The outstanding item Section 5.2 used to concede, a CDE-clustered
  bootstrap for the median, is done (SE 0.0093 against the asymptotic
  0.0076).
- Purpose of investment, a field in the public release that no earlier
  specification used, enters as a fourth decomposition block and raises the
  CDE share of the explained movement from 86% to 88.1%.
- A cross-model audit refuted one stated justification and one inference
  claim; both are corrected in the text and logged as D25 and D27.
